## Design Context

### Users

Zanwei and visitors to a public component playground: designers, engineers,
and collaborators who want to scan polished interaction experiments quickly,
open a focused preview, and follow the original source when they want detail.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and quietly playful. The interface should feel authored and
fast, with small moments of character that never compete with the work.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light-first editorial masonry gallery inspired by recent.design. Chrome is
minimal and neutral so each experiment can carry its own visual identity.
Motion should feel continuous and physical, using restrained transform and
opacity transitions rather than decorative effects.

### Design Principles

- Keep the showcased work dominant; navigation and metadata stay compact.
- Preserve spatial continuity when moving between a card and its playground.
- Prefer instant local previews and progressive loading over remote embeds.
- Make source provenance obvious without turning every card into chrome.
- Keep interactions keyboard-readable, responsive, and safe for reduced motion.
